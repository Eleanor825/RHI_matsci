# H14 Protocol Amendment 2

Date: 2026-08-22

Before running H14 on any external target, an audit of the already-consumed H13
development data showed that negative unique and extreme examples retain
continuous scientific utility, while the legacy gain multiplied all utility by
a binary top-decile/success label. That erased near-miss value and then also
charged failure harm and opportunity cost.

H14 therefore defines net scientific gain from continuous realized utility:

```text
G(a,o | s) = V(a,o) - c_exec(a) - h_fail(a,o) - rho_B(s).
```

The binary success label remains available for failure harm and risk reporting,
but no longer zeroes continuous utility. Pairwise negative examples already
have zero utility, so this correction cannot manufacture pairwise improvement.
Datasets, hashes, folds, alpha, delta, baseline families, and confirmatory gates
are unchanged. The previous hash is retained in `protocol-lock-v2.sha256`.
